#### Case Study 1 — Live Feature Development for a Retail Technology Software Vendor
*Current engagement · Rapid Prototyping · Software Engineering*

> Currently engaged as a subject matter expert and lead developer for an emerging planogram and retail planning software provider. Working at pace to build extensions and new capabilities that push their platform beyond its existing boundaries.
>
> The work has directly supported their sales process — rapidly prototyping and delivering live features during active client demos to major retailers, giving their prospects a real-time view of what the platform can do. New features, built on the spot, in the room.
>
> **This is rapid prototyping at its most high-stakes — and it's winning contracts.**

---

#### Case Study 2 — Building a Data & Analytics Function From the Ground Up
*Financial Services · Data Engineering · Team Leadership · Agile*

> Joined a leading financial services organisation with a single isolated analyst and no analytics function. Scaled the team to four, introduced Scrum, merged in the data engineering capability, migrated from Oracle to Snowflake, and moved reporting from Excel to Tableau.
>
> **One person. No structure. Full analytics function on the other side.**

---

#### Case Study 3 — Bridging the Gap: Spotfire & Snowflake Integration
*Market Research · Data Engineering · Software Engineering*

> In the early days of Snowflake's adoption, native connectors between Spotfire and Snowflake simply didn't exist. A specialist market research client needed to analyse large-scale medical questionnaire data — tracking the real-world effectiveness of drugs across patient populations — but had no way to surface that data meaningfully in their analytics platform.
>
> We designed and built a bespoke Node.js API engine to bridge the gap. The engine handled all communication between Spotfire and a custom Snowflake database schema, managing large-scale data calls efficiently and reliably. This created a fully functional market research layer above Spotfire — allowing analysts to query, filter and visualise complex medical response data at scale for the first time.
>
> The result was a working analytics capability where none had existed — enabling the client to generate meaningful insights from patient data and make informed decisions about drug efficacy, built on infrastructure that was ahead of what the platforms offered natively at the time.
